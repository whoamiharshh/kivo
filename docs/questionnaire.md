# Questionnaire — the last group expense you were part of

Six questions about something that already happened. No product involved, so the
answers are evidence rather than opinion about Pot.

Send it to people who have actually organised or paid into a group collection:
a trip, a fest or club fund, or a flat's shared costs. Ask about the **most
recent** one, not the worst one — "the worst" selects for drama and inflates
every number.

**Target: 20 responses before building any more screens.**

---

## The questions

Ask them in this order. The wording is chosen to get numbers rather than
feelings.

**1. What was the most recent thing you and a group put money together for, and
roughly how much per person?**
*(Open. Also tells you which of trip / fest / flat your tester belongs to.)*

**2. From the first time someone asked for money to the last person actually
paying — how many days was that?**
- Same day
- 2–3 days
- 4–7 days
- More than a week
- Someone never paid

**3. How many times did someone have to remind people?**
- Nobody needed reminding
- Once or twice
- Three to five times
- More than five
- Someone gave up reminding

**4. Who held the money before it was spent?**
- One person's bank account or UPI
- Split — people paid their own share directly to vendors
- The group's treasurer or office-bearer
- Cash with one person
- Other → ______

**5. Was there any confusion or disagreement about what the money was spent on,
or what happened to what was left over?**
- No, it was clear throughout
- Mild confusion, sorted out by talking
- A real argument
- We never worked out where the leftover went
- There was no leftover

**6. Did whoever held the money end up paying more than their share at any
point, even temporarily?**
- No
- Yes, a bit, and got it back
- Yes, and it took a while to get back
- Yes, and they never got all of it back
- I don't know

**Optional, one line:** if there was one annoying part of that whole thing,
what was it?

---

## What the answers decide

This is the point of asking before building more. Record the counts, then act on
them:

- **If Q2 and Q3 skew high** (days of delay, repeated reminders) → the headline
  is the deadline: money committed to a plan, with a date.
- **If Q4 is overwhelmingly "one person's account" and Q5/Q6 show friction** →
  the headline is custody: nobody has to hold the group's money.
- **If Q6 shows the holder regularly out of pocket** → the headline is that the
  pot fronts nothing and repays only from money already collected.
- **If none of these show a pattern** → the use case is wrong for this group.
  Ask what they *do* describe and change the copy before writing more screens.

## Recording the answers

Put the counts in `feedback-log.md` under "Questionnaire results", stating the
number of responses plainly. A question answered by 7 people is reported as
7 people, not as a percentage. Do not fill this in from guesses about what
friends would probably say — an empty section is honest, an invented one is not.
